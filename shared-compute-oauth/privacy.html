Privacy Policy

Application: Shared Compute OAuth

Operator: GD Global

Effective date: October 7, 2026

1. Purpose of Google Drive access

Shared Compute OAuth is a private utility used by GD Global's authorized operator to connect Google Drive with approved compute workflows and operator-controlled archival storage.

2. Data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files and folders needed for an authorized workflow. Depending on the workflow, it may read, create, update, move, or delete files under the operator's direction.

3. How data is used

Google Drive content is used only to execute authorized compute workflows and to transfer verified workflow inputs or outputs. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to build advertising profiles.

4. Sharing

Drive content is not disclosed to unrelated third parties. A workflow may transfer data to an external compute provider only when the authorized operator explicitly directs that workflow.

5. OAuth credentials

OAuth tokens and application credentials are stored only in operator-controlled secret stores and are used solely to authenticate Google Drive access. Credentials are not published in source repositories or task outputs.

6. Retention and deletion

Google Drive is used as temporary working storage for shared compute jobs. Temporary artifacts may be removed after verified transfer or when no longer required by the associated workflow. Long-term retention, when required, is maintained in operator-controlled archival storage.

7. Revoking access

The authorized Google Account holder can revoke the application's access at any time through the Google Account's connected-app or security settings.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to GD Global through the contact information published on the GD Global website.
