Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Shared Compute Pool rclone is a private utility used by GD Global's authorized operator to connect Google Drive with compute workflows and local archival storage.

Google Drive access

The application may read, create, move, and delete Google Drive files as directed by the authorized operator. Google Drive is used as temporary working storage for compute jobs. Files selected for long-term retention are transferred to operator-controlled storage.

Data handling

Google Drive content is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. The application does not provide Drive content to third parties except when the operator explicitly directs a workflow to an external service.

OAuth credentials

OAuth tokens and application credentials are stored on systems controlled by the operator and are used only to authenticate the application's Google Drive access. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's connected-app settings.

Retention

Temporary Drive artifacts may be removed after a verified transfer to operator-controlled archival storage. Local retention depends on the associated project and operational requirements.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact GD Global through its public GitHub profile.
