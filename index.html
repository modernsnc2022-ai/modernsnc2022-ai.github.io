HOW GD GLOBAL WORKS

Direct buyer–supplier trading. Clear intermediary terms.

Where agreed, buyer and supplier contract and settle payment directly. GD Global helps qualify and develop the opportunity, with supplier-paid commissions on initial and repeat transactions subject to written agreement. Introduced-customer registration and non-circumvention protection are agreed before identities are shared.

Commercial fees, account protection and any proposed three-year protection period are negotiated per case — not automatically guaranteed.
