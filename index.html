Services

Practical business development between Korea and global markets.

We do not operate as a traditional inventory-heavy importer. Our role is to qualify opportunities, connect the right counterparties, and support repeat transactions.

01 · Korea Market Development

For overseas manufacturers

Identify suitable Korean market segments, qualify potential industrial buyers, coordinate product and commercial discussions, and develop new accounts.

02 · Export Market Development

For Korean suppliers

Find overseas demand, qualify buyer requirements, support supplier-buyer introductions, and help structure repeat-order cooperation.

How we work

Supplier-first qualification. Buyer disclosure only when the structure is ready.

1. QualifyConfirm product capability, technical fit, export readiness and commercial terms.
2. ProtectAgree on account-conflict checks, protected-account principles and repeat-order commission.
3. IntroduceDisclose target market and buyer identity only at the appropriate stage.
4. DevelopSupport samples, quotations, technical discussion and the path to repeat orders.
