WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Documented answers, not just a list of names.

Outputs depend on the agreed scope. The examples below illustrate reporting formats, not completed customer projects or certified inspections.

Supplier research summary

Candidate company, official source links, product fit, outreach status, unanswered items and suggested next questions.

Supplier-contact log

Contact channel, date, attempted or completed conversation, supplier statement and what could not be confirmed.

Comparable RFQ matrix

Supplier-stated MOQ, unit pricing basis, Incoterms, sample terms, lead time and quote validity, when provided.

Evidence standards. We distinguish publicly supported facts, supplier claims and unknowns. A business-registration check is not proof of manufacturing capability, account ownership, product quality or performance.
CLEAR COMMERCIAL SCOPE

Free initial inquiry. Written scope before paid work.

We first review your business requirement and discuss whether local assistance is practical. Detailed supplier research, actual calls, repeated follow-ups, site visits and inspection coordination are not included in free consultation.

For paid work, the quotation states the named task, supplier or product scope, contact-attempt limits, expected deliverables, fee, approved expenses and limitations. Any additional work needs your agreement.

Examples of scoped assignments

Supplier check: one named company, available public records and authorized contact attempts.

Manufacturer shortlist: one product brief and research outcomes for potential Korean manufacturers.

New-order follow-up: one supplier and purchase order, with a defined reporting period.

Pricing is quoted individually after requirements and feasibility review. Site visits and independent inspections require separately confirmed capability and costs.
