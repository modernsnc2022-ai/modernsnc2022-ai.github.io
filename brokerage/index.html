HOW OUR BROKERAGE WORKS

Direct contracts. Clearly agreed introductions.

For buyers

Send product specifications, volume, delivery market and purchasing timeframe. We assess whether a relevant trading counterpart can be identified.

For suppliers

Share available product grades, minimum order, current export terms, origin and supporting documents. We evaluate suitability for actual buyer requirements.

Commission transparency

Any supplier-paid brokerage commission and protected-customer terms are negotiated separately in writing before a protected introduction. Terms are not assumed.

What you receive: a scoped introduction or a written update on matching progress, available supplier-stated information and unresolved commercial questions. No purchase funds pass through GD Global.
