GD Global Business Inquiry Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

What we collect

When you submit a Trade Brokerage or Korea Desk inquiry, you provide your business contact name/company, email, country, inquiry category and the purchasing or trading details you choose to share. Do not submit sensitive personal identifiers, confidential technical files or payment credentials in an initial inquiry.

Purpose and recipient

GD Global uses the information only to evaluate and respond to your request, prepare a proposed scope, and communicate about the requested business service. Messages are forwarded to marcusoh@gdglobal.kr.

Form delivery provider

The form transmits information to Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, for email forwarding. Web3Forms may process and temporarily retain submitted information under its own policies. Please use email instead if you do not wish to use this provider.

Retention and requests

GD Global retains business correspondence as needed for legitimate inquiry management, business records and legal obligations. Request access, correction or deletion, where applicable, by emailing marcusoh@gdglobal.kr.

Service scope

Sending an inquiry does not establish a paid contract. Any paid sourcing, local representation or brokerage terms require separate agreement.
